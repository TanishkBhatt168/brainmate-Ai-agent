5. BEGINNER-FRIENDLY METRIC EXPLANATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Accuracy:
  The percentage of test papers whose categories were correctly predicted out of all test papers.
• Precision (Macro):
  Of all papers the model predicted as category X, what fraction actually belonged to X? Averaged
  equally across all categories.
• Recall (Macro):
  Of all papers that truly belonged to category X, what fraction did the model successfully find?
  Averaged equally across all categories.
• Macro F1-Score:
  The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall calculated per class, then averaged across all classes.
  Crucial for imbalanced data because it treats rare categories equally with massive ones.
• Weighted F1-Score:
  F1-score averaged across classes with weights proportional to each category's test sample size.
